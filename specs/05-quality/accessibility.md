# Accessibility Specification

## Interaction

- Logical, visible focus order across binder, editor, inspector, dialogs, and
  toolbars.
- Keyboard equivalents for create, rename, move, search, view switching, and
  compile.
- No keyboard trap in editor or composition mode.

## Semantics

- Tree, table, tab, toolbar, and document roles expose current state.
- Card position, label, status, selection, and drag destination have non-color
  representations.
- Live regions announce completed saves, search result changes, and compile
  outcomes without excessive interruption.

## Visual

- Text scales without clipping critical controls.
- Light/dark themes meet the approved contrast target.
- Reduced-motion settings disable nonessential animation.
