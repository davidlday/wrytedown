# Product Vision

## Problem

Long-form authors need to plan, research, draft, restructure, review, and
publish a large work without forcing all content into one linear document and
without being bound to proprietary digital formats.

## Vision

I envision a future where writers permanently own their work, creative content
outlives software vendors, and professional writing tools are accessible to
everyone through open standards and open-source technology.

Wrytedown provides writers with an open-source, cross-platform writing
environment that preserves ownership of creative work through open formats and
sustainable software design. It is a project-centered writing workspace where
authors can break a manuscript into independently editable items, arrange those
items hierarchically, explore their relationships visually, inspect them through
multiple views, keep research beside the draft, and produce their manuscripts in
a variety of formats to suit their needs.

## Product principles

- Structure and prose are separate but synchronized.
- Authors may plan first, draft first, or mix both approaches.
- Source content remains independent of export presentation.
- Every major operation must be understandable and reversible where practicable.
- Large projects remain navigable through search, metadata, collections, and
  focused views.
