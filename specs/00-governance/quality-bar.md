# Quality Bar

## Required gates

- Every requirement has an ID, owner, status, and acceptance evidence.
- No destructive operation ships without a recovery story.
- Persistence changes include migration and rollback tests.
- Compile output is deterministic for identical source, settings, and
  application version.
- Keyboard-only operation is supported for all MVP workflows.
- User-generated content is never transmitted without explicit action and
  disclosure.
- Projects remain readable after an interrupted save through atomic persistence
  or recovery.
